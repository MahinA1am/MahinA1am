## 👋 Hi, I'm Mahin Alam

🤖 **AI engineer** building production LLM systems: agents that call tools, evaluation pipelines that weigh models on cost and quality, and the backend that keeps them reliable.
🎓 B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CGPA 3.99/4.00), with a background in machine learning for health and vision.

---

### 🛠️ What I Work On
- 🔌 **Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers** that let AI assistants such as Claude act inside a product, with OAuth sign-in, sessions and tool safety annotations
- 📊 **LLM evaluation**: replaying real data through competing models and scoring the outputs to choose the cheapest model that holds quality
- ✍️ **Multi-stage LLM pipelines**: scraping, an LLM quality gate, embeddings and vector search (pgvector), generation, and LLM-as-judge scoring
- 🎙️ **AI meeting notetakers**: calendar-driven recording bots, transcripts, and action items extracted by an LLM
- 💳 **Usage-based billing** on Stripe with an idempotent credit ledger that meters every model and tool call

Most of this work lives in private company repositories. My public projects are below.
